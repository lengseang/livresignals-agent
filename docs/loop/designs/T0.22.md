# T0.22 — the real-money book's +1.05%/trade is five trades deep

**Status:** research only (read-only probes + this note). No rule, no gate, no
order path touched. Branch `loop/T0.22-trend-tail-probe`, cut from
`loop/T0.21-cohort-of-one`.

**Why this and not a board task.** The pickable chain is still blocked
(T0.10→E10, T0.12→E11, T0.14→E13, T0.15→E14, T0.20→E16). The two shifts before
this one worked the *paper unified* book. The **real-money** book
(`trend_follower.py`, `data/trend-state.json` `log`) has never had its
distribution written down, and two shipped reports print its arithmetic mean as
the headline number:

```text
scripts/expectancy_vs_fee.py:11
  trend_follower (REAL $)  n=212  avg gross +1.210%  net/trade +1.050%
                           t=+1.68  win 21.7%  -> "alive, unproven"
scripts/per_symbol_net.py  (ALL BOOKS COMBINED, per-trade column)
```

## The measurement

`research/_trend_tail_concentration.py` — method declared in the file before
the numbers were read. Population: every closed row, no exclusions. Net = gross
− 0.1594% round trip (the repo's fee basis).

```text
n=212  win 21.7%  mean gross +1.210%  mean NET +1.050%
median NET -1.324%
sum gross +256.5%   t=+1.68 (descriptive only: fat right tail)

DELETE-k-BEST   (drop k largest winners, re-measure NET mean)
  drop 1: mean NET +0.650%      drop 2: +0.472%
  drop 3: +0.295%               drop 4: +0.140%
  drop 5: +0.003%   <- the whole edge, gone

top 11 rows (5% of trades) supply 54% of every dollar won.
```

The five trades that carry it are +85.7% (NEAR), +38.1% (TIA), +37.7% (AR),
+32.6% (SEI), +28.2% (APT) — all `trend-broke` exits, all banked between
2026-10-18 and 2026-10-30. The book wins one in five and its median trade loses
1.3% after fees. That is a legitimate trend-following *shape*; it is not an
183-trade base.

**Window check.** 28d and 30d windows were computed first and rejected as a
headline: both contain the whole October run, so they flatter the book (28d
NET +2.63%). The honest recent windows:

```text
last 14d  n=25  mean NET +2.547%  sum +67.7% gross
          its 3 best trades = +151.5% gross -> the other 22 rows = -83.8%
last  7d  n= 6  mean NET -7.218%  sum -42.4% gross  (ONE twice, -16.7%/-10.2%)
```

So the last 14 days are NEAR +85.7%, TIA +38.1%, INJ +27.7% and 22 losers.

## Two defects found while measuring (both need owner action or a design)

### 1. The trend book honours no edge disable — `validated` is live in it

`unified_trader._edge_disabled()` is the single reader for
`data/signals-state.json` `disabled`, and it is enforced on the paper openers
only. `trend_follower.py` has **no reader for that file at all** (grep across
`scripts/` and `src/`: no hit). The same list is **currently deployed**
(`data/signals-state.json` `disabled` contains `validated`); in the real book
`real.closed` 11 of 18 rows carry edge `validated` (the older rows carry none),
and the paper book's p2 line says `validated` is `DECAYING … tighten its entry`.
The trend log itself records **no edge field on any of its 212 rows**, so a
per-edge verdict cannot be computed on it without the reader inferring one.

Nothing here is safe to change from the loop: `trend_follower.py` is on the
never-edit list, and applying a paper-venue verdict to a real-money opener is a
strategy decision. **Escalated as E17** with the one-line shape of a fix and
the reason the naive fix is wrong (an empty `disabled` list means "off", and a
four-shift paper verdict is not evidence for a real book).

### 2. The NEAR row that carries 29% of the book's gross cannot be reproduced

```text
trend-state.json  NEAR/USDT  entry 2.376 -> exit 4.412  ret +0.8569  2026-10-29T04:10Z
logs/trend.log 2026-10-29 04:10Z
  EXIT NEAR/USDT @ 4.412     <- 1.2 s later, a SECOND NEAR exit at 4.971
  EXIT NEAR/USDT @ 4.971
2026-10-29 20:10Z  OPEN NEAR/USDT @ 4.677   <- re-entered at 4.677
```

* `data/trend-state.json` `real.closed` holds the **real** NEAR trade:
  `entry 2.369 → exit 4.971, qty 2.3, NO_SELLABLE_BALANCE`. The `log` row is a
  **different trade** (paper, entry 2.376, exit 4.412).
* `logs/trend.log` at 2026-10-29 04:10Z lists **two** NEAR exits 1.2 s apart
  (`@ 4.412`, then `@ 4.971`), then the 20:10Z run opens NEAR `@ 4.677`.
  Exit 4.971 → open 4.677 → exit 4.327 is a chain in which the re-entry sits
  below the previous exit — a chain `coin_trend()` does not produce.

The paper truth was cross-read from `unified-paper.json`, which records every
NEAR fill through 2026-10-30: 4.65, 4.56, 4.60, 4.66, 4.57, 4.56, 4.49, 4.39,
**4.34**, then 4.57, 4.41, 4.19, 4.13, **4.11**, 4.05, 4.29. NEAR never came
near 2.37 in that window, and no fill sits between 4.34 and 4.57. The recorded
+85.7% is therefore either a wrong entry price or a corrupted row — and it is
**29% of the book's total gross** and the entire basis of the `mean +1.05%`.

**Do not "correct" the row from here.** The loop has no 1h/4h/1d candles for
NEAR in `trading.db` (it stores 10 symbols only) and writing a reconstructed
entry would be a synthetic number, which LOOP.md §3 forbids. What is needed is
the exchange's own fill history for that order (owner has the key) — a 10-minute
check that either restores 29% of the book or removes it.

## What this does NOT say

* It does not say the trend rule has no edge. The delete-1 mean is still
  +0.65%, and the same window shows a real trend signature (many small losses,
  a handful of large wins).
* It does not license exit tuning. Any TP/SL fitted to these 212 rows is fitted
  to five of them; this is the same conclusion `self_improve`'s optimizer
  reached from the other side on 2026-11-11 ("no exit tuning can rescue it").
* It does not change the `t=+1.68` verdict — `expectancy_vs_fee.py` already
  says `alive, unproven`, which this measurement now supports with a reason.

## Adversarial self-review ("it is 3 months later and this failed: why?")

* *"You picked 14d because it was unflattering."* The 14d cut was chosen after
  seeing 28d/30d (both flattering) — the note says so, and both are printed in
  the probe source's comment. The 7d cut is also shown.
* *"Delete-5 is a stunt; you can always kill a mean."* Right, which is why the
  delete-1 mean (+0.65%) is the headline and the delete-5 is the bound. The
  decision-relevant number is the NEAR row: 29% of gross in one row whose entry
  price disagrees with every other record of that symbol in the window.
* *"The 2-exit-in-one-run theory is unproven."* It is — the note reports the
  two log lines and the price chain and stops there rather than naming a code
  line it cannot prove.
