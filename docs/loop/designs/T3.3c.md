# T3.3c · Design and PRE-REGISTRATION: the trend rule as it actually trades (`trend_real`)

Status: pre-registered **before any `trend_real` run**. This file was committed before the first replay of the
plugin; the grid, windows, costs and reporting below are fixed and are not changed after seeing results.
Research only (mode `REPLAY`); nothing here places, sizes or manages a real order. `scripts/trend_follower.py`
was read, never imported, run or edited.

## Why

The `trend_sma` baseline (2 coins, daily checks, no stop, BTC filter = SMA_n) FAILED the gate: DSR 0.8898 < 0.95
at N = 4 (`data/local/replay/20260926T014518Z-trend_sma/gate.md`). It also differed from the live rule in five
ways listed in its own caveats. Before calling the trend idea dead or alive, replay the rule **as traded**.

A consequence of R8 + R12 worth stating: a coin whose paper entry fired while the 3 slots were full is **not**
bought later when a slot frees; it waits for its paper state to exit and re-enter. The port keeps this.

## Port (`src/trading/replay/plugins/trend_real.py`)

- Subscribes to `1m` and `1d`. It acts only on the BTC 1m bar that first reaches a check instant
  `k * 4h + 10 min` (robust to the phase-shifted 2017-18 bars and to gaps); all 15 coins are evaluated at that
  instant from `ctx`, exactly as the live loop iterates `SYMS`.
- Series (R3) = completed 1d bars (last 299) + the latest 1m close as the forming candle. If a coin has no 1m bar
  since the start of the current UTC day, the coin is skipped for that check (no forming candle).
- The real book is read from the account (`ctx.position`), so warm-up signals (discarded by the engine) cannot
  desync it. The paper state (R8, R10) is internal to the plugin, as in the live state file.
- Every rule constant is in `params` (so the T3.3b trial key sees it): `sma`, `btc_sma` 200, `hard_stop`,
  `max_pos`, `min_bars` 60, `btc_min_bars` 205, `mom_days` 30, `max_daily_bars` 300, `check_every_h` 4,
  `check_offset_min` 10, `universe`.
- Engine change needed for R13: at one instant, every account fills pending **sells before buys** across all
  symbols (before, fills went in symbol order, so a buy of `ADA` could be refused a slot that the same-instant sale
  of `SOL` was freeing). It changes nothing for `trend_sma` (slots = symbols, never binds).

### Known deviations from live (stated, not fixed)

1. Sizing: the replay's shared account, `min(cash, equity / 3)` per entry (compounding), not the live fixed
   $5.50 (`:53`, `:294`). The live `free_usdt >= 5.01` check (`:289`) and the $5 min-notional bump (`:296-297`)
   are not modelled. Per-trade returns (the DSR observations) do not depend on sizing; account returns do.
2. Fill: next 1m open after the check (live: a market order seconds after 00:10). Live wallet is shared with other
   books (UNKNOWN balances); the replay account is dedicated.
3. For SMA_N > 60 variants the coin needs `max(60, N)` bars. The live code would compute `sum(last k)/N` with
   `k < N` on a young coin (`:58-59`), a latent defect; the variants do not replicate it. For N = 50 (live) there
   is no difference.
4. Spreads measured in 2026 (7.56 h sample, `spreads_v1.json`) are applied to 2017-2026; early spreads were
   probably wider, so early costs are likely understated (direction known, size UNKNOWN).
5. Universe is today's survivors (survivorship bias); coins enter when their Binance data starts
   (BNB 2017-11, LTC 2017-12, ADA 2018-04, XRP 2018-05, LINK 2019-01, ATOM 2019-04, DOGE 2019-07, DOT/SOL 2020-08,
   AVAX 2020-09, NEAR 2020-10, ARB 2023-03, SUI 2023-05); no backfill, no interpolation.

## PRE-REGISTERED run plan

- **Variant grid (8):** `SMA_N ∈ {50, 100, 150, 200}` × `hard_stop ∈ {0.15, off}`. Everything else as traded
  (BTC SMA200, MAX_POS 3, 4 h at :10). The live configuration is `SMA_N = 50, hard_stop = 0.15`.
- **Data:** 1m klines, `D:\1111-Works\LIVRsignals\livresignals-agent\data\local\history\monthly`.
- **Windows:** warm-up from 2017-08, trading 2017-11-01 → 2026-03-01; **IS 2017-11 → 2022-01, OOS 2022-01 → 2026-03**.
  Holdout (from 2026-03) **not opened**.
- **Costs:** `--broker paper`: measured per-symbol spreads (median = net, p90 = stress), fee 0.10 %/side taker
  (the account's real rate is UNKNOWN), `--fill-cap-usd none` (labelled `depth_unmodelled`, see deviation 1).
  A symbol without a measured spread is refused (`UnknownSpreadError`), never guessed; all 15 are measured today.
- **Start equity:** 1000 USDT (scale-free), 3 slots.
- **Trials:** every variant appends to `data/local/replay/trials.jsonl`; the gate runs on each with N = the distinct
  parameter sets in the whole log (T3.3b), which includes the 4 earlier `trend_sma` sets because they are part of
  the same search: expected N = 4 + 8 = 12.
- **Report:** all 8 variants, per variant: OOS net return, OOS max drawdown, OOS daily Sharpe (annualised,
  informational), OOS per-trade Sharpe and DSR, closed trades (full and OOS), BTC buy&hold OOS net, gate verdict.
  No variant is picked as "the" result.

## Failure modes (pre-mortem)

- Check timing off by one bar: covered by a test on the check grid.
- The real book drifting from the account: the plugin reads the account instead of keeping its own.
- Same-instant sell/buy ordering: covered by an engine test.
- Runtime: 15 symbols × ~4.5 M instants; each variant is a separate process.

## Tests

`tests/loop/test_trend_real.py` (RED first): check grid; forming candle; entry needs BTC > SMA200; the BTC filter
does not exit; edge-triggered entries; ranking by 30-day momentum under MAX_POS; hard stop on/off; min bars;
params carry every constant; engine fills sells before buys at one instant.

## RESULTS (added 2026-09-26 AFTER the runs; nothing above was changed)

**REPLAY / PAPER, simulated. Research, not evidence of edge.** All 8 pre-registered variants, 15 coins, 1m history,
trading 2017-11-01 → 2026-03-01, IS/OOS split 2022-01, paper broker (median spread net, p90 stress, 0.10 %/side,
uncapped, `depth_unmodelled`), 1000 USDT, 3 slots. Holdout (2026-03 on) not opened. Each run ~3,650 s.

Two things surfaced while running, both fixed with tests before the results below were produced:
1. The loader raised on `LTCUSDT-1m-2018-02` (a real last-before-halt bar closing 999 ms late). A scan of all
   1,268 zips found 5 such rows; they are now kept and counted, and one that overlaps its successor
   (SOLUSDT 2021-12-24) is dropped and counted (`data_anomalies` in every summary).
2. The gate could not price a paper-broker run (it expected the flat model's `fee_rate`). It now uses the taker
   fee plus the worst measured spread among the run's symbols (a conservative bound; shown as `cost_basis`).

**Verdict: FAIL for all 8 at N = 12.** Every variant beats BTC buy&hold OOS on return, but none survives the
selection correction. Why the DSR is so low: SR0 = 0.1804 per trade, set by the spread of the 12 trial Sharpes
(V = 0.01175; the 2-coin daily `trend_sma` trials had per-trade SR 0.20-0.39, these 0.06-0.12); and the per-trade
returns are extremely fat-tailed (skew 8.8-11.9, kurtosis 90-159: a 12-13 % win rate carried by a few very large
winners), which widens the PSR denominator. Even against zero (no deflation) PSR is 0.92-0.998.

Facts worth recording:
- The hard stop barely matters: it fired 5-9 times per variant out of 190-563 exits; trend breaks do the work.
- Stress (p90 spreads) costs almost nothing: measured spreads on these 15 pairs are 0.001-8.4 bp; the fee dominates.
- The live configuration (SMA50 + stop) has the highest IS return and the weakest OOS per-trade SR of the grid;
  IS/OOS decay is large (+2718 % IS vs +82 % OOS, with a -63 % OOS drawdown).

**Sensitivity, NOT the verdict (chosen after seeing results, so it cannot upgrade anything):** if N and V[SR_n]
used only the 8 `trend_real` variants (V = 0.000704, SR0 = 0.0387), DSR would be SMA50 0.761 / 0.755,
SMA100 0.697 / 0.700, SMA150 0.881 / 0.881, SMA200 0.977 / 0.971 (stop / off). The live SMA50 fails either way.
Treating SMA200 as a candidate would need a fresh pre-registration and the untouched holdout, not this sample.

Caveats that bound all of the above: survivorship (today's 15 coins), 2026 spreads applied to 2017, compounding
sizing instead of the live $5.50, depth not modelled, the account's real fee rate UNKNOWN, and SMA50 itself was
chosen by the live script's author on overlapping history (N is a lower bound).
