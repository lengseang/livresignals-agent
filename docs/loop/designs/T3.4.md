# T3.4 · Design and pre-registration: the audit's TEST strategies through the gate

Status: design + **pre-registration**. This file is committed before any variant below is replayed; the commit
history is the proof of order. **REPLAY/PAPER only.** Nothing here places, sizes or manages a real order, and no
result produced under this design is evidence of edge. Short positions are a **PAPER-ONLY simulation**: the
account trades spot, which cannot short.

Inputs: `docs/research/strategy-audit.md` (rows #2, #8, #9 are TEST), `docs/research/fees-and-breakeven.md`
(TP ≤ 0.3% is not viable at VIP 0; the viable zone is TP ≥ ~1% or asymmetric R), `docs/research/claims-vs-fees.md`
(regime-adaptive L/S is "the best current candidate", its claim is unverified), `docs/loop/designs/T3.0-T3.1.md`
(replay harness), `docs/loop/designs/T4.1.md` (fill model, kline mode incl. stop/take-profit rules),
`src/trading/replay/gate.py` (T3.3 gate).

## Problem

The audit selected three idea families to test: intraday momentum/reversal at hourly horizons (#2), range breakout
with volume confirmation (#8), and regime-adaptive long/short (#9). None has been run through our own replay with
our own costs. Two pieces are missing in the harness:

1. **Stop-loss / take-profit / time-stop exits.** The replay `Signal` has no SL/TP fields, and the replay broker
   never calls the kline-mode helpers (`trading.paper.fills.bar_exit`). Intraday targets are only viable with
   asymmetric exits (fees-and-breakeven.md), so these must be modelled with the conservative T4.1 rules.
2. **Short positions** (regime_ls bear leg). The account is long-only.
3. The gate raises `KeyError: 'fee_rate'` on any run made with the paper broker (`--broker paper`), because
   `round_trip_cost` only understands the legacy flat cost model. Found while reading; fixed minimally.

## Harness changes (minimal, backwards compatible)

- `Signal` gains three optional fields, default `None` (every existing construction is unchanged):
  `sl` and `tp` (fractional distances from the entry fill's reference price, e.g. 0.01 = 1%) and `max_hold_ms`.
- The replay broker (`PaperAccount`, inherited by `KlineBroker`), on an `open` fill, stores
  `stop = ref × (1 ∓ sl)`, `take_profit = ref × (1 ± tp)` and `deadline = fill time + max_hold_ms`, where `ref` is the
  fill bar's open (the mid the fill model starts from).
- At every 1m close (`mark`), a held position with a stop/TP is checked against that 1m bar with the T4.1 kline
  rules: `KlineBroker` calls `fills.bar_exit` itself; the legacy account applies the same rule through its own
  pricing hooks. Stops are taker orders (gap-through fills at the open, minus half the spread); take-profits are
  limits that need a strict trade-through and fill at the limit; **a bar touching both books the stop**. The
  entry's own 1m bar is checked too: the fill happened at its open, so its high/low came at or after the fill.
- Time stop: at the first 1m close at or after the deadline, the broker queues a market close, filled at the
  **next 1m open** like any close. It is a broker event, not a strategy signal.
- Fee on a take-profit fill is the maker fee, on every other fill the taker fee (both 0.10% here).
- **Short positions (PAPER ONLY).** `Signal(side="S", action="open")` opens a short: the slot budget is posted as
  collateral, `qty = budget / (sell_price × (1 + fee))`; its value is `qty × (2 × entry − mark)`; closing buys back
  at the buy price and pays the fee. `ctx.position()` returns a **negative** qty for a short (longs unchanged).
  Not modelled, and stated on every short-enabled result: borrow/funding cost (UNKNOWN; perpetual funding can be
  either sign), margin calls and liquidation (a short losing more than its collateral is not force-closed).
- `run.py` gains `--strategy <registry name>`, `--params <json>` and `--tag <text>` (to keep parallel run ids
  distinct); the defaults reproduce the existing trend_sma run exactly. Plugin-specific caveats come from the
  plugin (`caveats` attribute) instead of being hard-coded for trend_sma.
- `gate.round_trip_cost` accepts the paper-broker cost header: `c = 2 × (fee_taker + half spread)`, with the
  spread of the **worst** (widest) symbol in the run at the run's `spread_stat`, i.e. the conservative p*.

## The strategies (rules fixed here, before any run)

All entries are market orders filled at the next 1m open after the signal bar closes (the harness rule). All
bars are resampled from 1m (UTC-aligned) and a strategy only sees closed bars (`ctx`). "Contiguous" below means
the span between the first and last bar used equals the expected number of intervals; if a bar is missing
(exchange outage), no signal is emitted for that bar. A held symbol is never re-entered; re-entry after an exit
is allowed on the next signal.

### 1. `intraday_momentum` and `intraday_reversal` (audit #2, Wen et al. 2022, hourly horizon)

On each **1h** close of coin X (long-only spot):

- `r_L = close / close[L bars ago] − 1`; `σ` = sample std of the last 168 hourly simple returns (one week);
  `z = r_L / (σ × √L)`.
- **momentum:** enter long if `z ≥ +2.0`. **reversal:** enter long if `z ≤ −2.0` (buy the fall).
- Exits: stop-loss `sl`, take-profit `tp = 2 × sl` (asymmetric R), time stop `max_hold`. Nothing else.

The paper's effect is intraday (first half-hour predicts last half-hour); this is the hourly-horizon version the
audit asked for, not a replication of the paper.

### 2. `range_breakout` (audit #8)

On each close of the strategy's interval (1h or 4h) of coin X (long-only spot):

- Range = the previous `N` bars (excluding the current one). Enter long if `close > max(high of the range)` **and**
  `volume > 2.0 × mean(volume of the range)`.
- Exits: `sl`, `tp = 2 × sl`, `max_hold`.

### 3. `regime_ls` (audit #9; a port of the rule documented in `scripts/regime_trader.py`, read-only, not imported)

Once per **UTC day**, on BTC's daily close (every symbol's daily bar for that day is already in the store when any
strategy runs: same-instant fairness, T3.0 §3.6):

- Regime: **bull** if BTC close > SMA_R(BTC) daily, else **bear**.
- Exits, for each held position: a long closes if its close < SMA50; a short closes if its close > SMA50
  ("trend broke"). Time stop 21 days (`max_hold`), optional hard stop `sl` / take-profit `tp` in the broker.
- Entries, only into slots that were free **before** today's exits (a slot freed today is reused tomorrow; this
  avoids an order-of-fill artefact in the shared account):
  - bull: candidates = coins with close > SMA50; rank by 30-day return, **strongest** first; open **long**.
  - bear: candidates = coins with close < SMA50; rank by 30-day return, **weakest** first; open **short (PAPER ONLY)**.
- The universe is every symbol whose daily bar the strategy has seen (so a coin joins on the day after its first
  daily bar; it also needs 50 days for SMA50 and 31 for the 30-day return). BTC itself is a candidate.
- `topn = 3` positions (the script's `TOPN`), equal slot sizing from the shared account.

Differences from the live script, stated rather than hidden: no ATR stop and no risk-parity sizing (equal slots),
no breadth / BTC-momentum gate (`USE_GATE`), no gold slot, no macro filter, Binance spot history instead of Bybit
linear, and the script's SMA_R has been both 200 (docstring) and 150 (current constant); both are in the grid.

### Amendment 1 (before any result): late-closing traded 1m rows

The first launch of all 16 variants stopped after ~30 s, before any trial was logged or any result existed:
the T3.1 loader raised on `LTCUSDT-1m-2018-02` (open 1518321540000, close_time 1518321600999, 28 trades), a
row closing 1 s past its minute. BTC and ETH, the only coins T3.1 scanned, never have this. A timestamp-only
scan of all 15 coins (months before the holdout) found **5 such traded rows**, each 0.1-1.5 s late: LTC 2018-02,
LINK/NEAR/SOL 2021-04-25 04:00, SOL 2021-12-24. (Every coin also has the known zero-trade 2020-12-21
placeholder, already dropped.) Options: keep the real end (overlaps the next bar and breaks the engine's
ordering invariant), clamp the end to the minute (leaks up to 1.5 s of the future into 5 bars), or **drop the
row** (a 1-minute data gap, no look-ahead). Chosen: drop and count as `dropped_late_close` (tolerance 2 s;
anything later still raises), reported in each run's `data_anomalies`. The grid, universe, windows and costs
are unchanged.

## Gate (pre-registered)

- After **all 16** runs finish, `python -m trading.replay.gate --run <dir>` on each. N = every line of
  `data/local/replay/trials.jsonl` at that moment: the 5 earlier trend_sma trials plus these 16 (21 if nothing else
  was logged meanwhile; the actual count is reported). The same N is used for every gate verdict.
- DSR observations = OOS closed trades (net per-trade returns). Verdicts: PASS / FAIL / INSUFFICIENT_DATA.
- `params` carry `sl`/`tp`, **not** the gate's `exits` key: these exits are not fixed (time stops and the SMA50
  cross end trades between SL and TP), so the gate's mean-net-per-trade test applies, not "win rate > p*". The
  report still prints each variant's net win rate **beside its break-even p\***, computed with the T1.1 formula
  `p* = (sl + c) / (tp + sl)`, `c = 2 × (fee + half spread)` (0.200% on BTC … 0.284% on DOT at the median spread):

| sl / tp | p* (BTC … DOT) |
|---|---|
| 1% / 2% | 40.0% … 42.8% |
| 2% / 4% | 36.7% … 38.1% |
| 4% / 8% | 35.0% … 35.7% |
| 10% / 20% | 34.0% … 34.3% |

- Reported for every variant: OOS net return, OOS max DD, OOS closed trades, net win rate vs p*, mean net per
  trade, BTC buy-and-hold OOS net, stress (p90) OOS net, regime, gate verdict. Full-window numbers too.

## Options considered

| Option | Why not (or why) |
|---|---|
| Strategy-side exits (the plugin watches 1m bars and emits `close`) | exits would fill at the next open after the bar that crossed the level, which is neither the stop's gap-through rule nor the TP's limit rule from T4.1; and every plugin would reimplement it. The broker applies the one audited rule |
| Fixed-percent thresholds for momentum entries | a 2% move in 4h means different things for BTC in 2018 and SUI in 2024; the z-score over a trailing week is a scale-free, look-ahead-free alternative |
| Tune thresholds on IS, then test OOS | would multiply trials; the grid is small, fixed in advance, and all of it is reported |
| Model perp funding for shorts | no funding history on disk (sources.md); inventing a number breaks rule 2. Stated as UNKNOWN instead |
| Reduce the universe for speed | not needed: the timing run shows ~15-25 min per variant |

## Pre-mortem: it is 3 months later and this failed. Why?

1. **A variant passed by luck and got deployed.** Guard: N counts all 21+ trials; DSR; the stress (p90) run is
   printed; one replay is research, not evidence; the holdout stays closed for a later final gate.
2. **The stop/TP rule flattered results** (e.g. TP filled on a touch, or the stop checked before the fill).
   Guard: tests for both-hit → stop, gap-through, trade-through, and that exits never use a bar that opened
   before the fill.
3. **Shorts looked profitable because costs of shorting were ignored.** Guard: every short-enabled result is
   labelled PAPER-ONLY with funding/borrow UNKNOWN and no liquidation; the long side is reported separately where
   possible (per-trade side in the events).
4. **Look-ahead in a rolling window** (e.g. the range including the current bar, σ including the signal bar's
   future). Guard: per-plugin no-look-ahead tests: changing any bar after time T never changes signals at or
   before T, and the recording canary never sees `end_ms > now`.
5. **Parallel runs corrupted the trial log.** Guard: distinct run ids (`--tag`), and the log is parsed line by line
   after the runs (every line must be valid JSON and every run id present exactly once).
6. **Survivorship.** The 15 coins are today's survivors; stated on every result.
7. **Spread in bp from a 2026 sample applied to 2017-2022** (T4.1): stated; p90 stress printed.

## Test plan (tests/loop/test_t34_strategies.py, written first; RED recorded before code)

- `Signal` built without the new fields still works; new fields default to None.
- Broker: long SL, long TP (strict trade-through), both-hit books the stop, gap-through fills at the open, time
  stop fills at the next 1m open, TP uses the maker fee; `KlineBroker` exits equal `fills.bar_exit` prices.
- Broker shorts: entry/exit arithmetic by hand, equity marking, `ctx.position` negative qty, short SL/TP.
- Each plugin on tiny synthetic bars: fires exactly where the rule says, not elsewhere; gap → no signal.
- No-look-ahead per plugin: perturbing all bars after T leaves every signal at or before T unchanged; the
  recording canary never observes the future.
- Gate: `round_trip_cost` on a paper-broker header uses the worst symbol's spread.
- Registry: the four plugins are registered, build, and point at this design.
- `run.py --strategy <name> --params <json>` runs end to end on synthetic history.
