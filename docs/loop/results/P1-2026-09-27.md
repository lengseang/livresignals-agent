## Against the pre-registered hypotheses
- **A1: as predicted.** It beats both benchmarks on risk-adjusted terms with a fraction of the drawdown
  (-9% vs -67% for BTC) but does not clear the deflation bar (0.817 < 0.95). Low exposure (25% vol target,
  1/K per coin) means small absolute returns: +161% over 2017-2026 vs BTC +934%. It is drawdown control,
  which is what the independent evidence says trend following delivers.
- **A2: failed badly.** Negative even before costs. A 15-coin cross-section is a weak test of a 3,000-coin
  factor; this rejects our adaptation, not the paper.
- **A3: as predicted.** Before costs it beats BTC (0.47 vs 0.42); daily rebalancing (10,218 fills) costs
  enough to push it below (0.29). Drawdown is smaller than BTC's (-47% vs -67%) but not halved.

## Pipeline finding (no verdict changes)
The per-trade checks count trades *opened and closed inside OOS*. Target-weight strategies keep one position
open for years and trim it, so they show **0 OOS trades** and could never PASS those checks. No verdict here
depends on it (A1 and A3 fail the DSR anyway). A rule for target-weight strategies (for example, OOS rebalance
weeks instead of closed trades) must be pre-registered before the next batch; owner decision.
